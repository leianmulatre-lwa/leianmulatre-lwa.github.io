Frequently asked questions

Is BIG LWA an Adobe Photoshop or Illustrator clone?

No. lightR and paintR serve some overlapping image and vector tasks, but they are independent editors with different capabilities.

Can I import PowerPoint into visualslidez?

Yes. BIG LWA includes a PowerPoint importer that preserves the original file. Browser previews are best-effort and may not reproduce all animations, fonts or media.

Are the games free?

The BIG LWA Games page includes browser-based activities. Third-party destinations may have their own terms or account requirements.

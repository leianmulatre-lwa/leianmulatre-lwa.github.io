How to check a journey

  1. Enter pickup and dropoff

    Use a public place or approximate location. You don't need to publish your home address.

  2. Explore the route

    Compare the on-map road distance and approximate time; start BIG LWA navigation only if you choose.

  3. Choose where to book

    Inspect ride or delivery choices in the Map and open the actual provider only for the final price or transaction.

About live fare estimates

Rideshare fares depend on current supply, demand, pickup conditions, vehicle type and provider rules. BIG LWA does not receive authorized live quote data yet, so it does not label a route-model travel time as an Uber fare or a Lyft pickup ETA. Similarly, a courier estimate would require an approved Uber Direct or DoorDash Drive partnership. These are integration targets, not live features.

For a real price, use the provider's final quote. The Map can still help you decide where you're going and see which services you want to check.

Location privacy

Do not publish precise home or private-person addresses in public map pins. Public discovery of nearby businesses is user-initiated. Booking with an outside provider follows that provider's privacy and pricing policies.

Questions people ask

Does BIG LWA have a live Uber fare checker?

Not yet. The Map can show a model-based route and route time, but Uber's live prices and pickup ETAs require approved integration. The final provider handoff displays an actual quote.

Can I compare food delivery fees?

BIG LWA lists provider options and lets you discover restaurants; it does not presently fetch live fees, menus or estimated delivery times.

Can I follow turn-by-turn navigation?

The Map includes an in-page navigation workflow with foreground permission. It is not the same as Uber navigation or live traffic routing.

The person behind the platform

Leian Mulatre.

Also known as Leian Stanley.

Creator, creative director, and founder of BIG LWA. One person with a lot of interests, building a place that makes room for all of yours.

People. Ideas. Sounds. Self.

BIG LWA is built around one belief: you shouldn't have to cut yourself into pieces to belong somewhere. Art, music, memory, research, conversation, and whatever you become next all deserve room to exist together.

My work moves between creative direction, culture, education, and technology. BIG LWA is where those worlds meet: a platform for making, finding, keeping, and sharing on your own terms.

A bigger you. Brighter worlds.

Privacy should be the bare minimum. Your work should still belong to you. And the internet can be a little more human than it is now.

Privacy Policy · September 7, 2026

Privacy should feel like shelter.

BIGLWA is designed around the idea that your identity, likeness, relationships, and creative work are not raw material for someone else to exploit.

What we collect right now

For account access, BIGLWA uses Firebase Authentication and may process your email address, username, authentication identifiers, and basic account status. Studio appearance choices such as wallpaper and widget styling are currently stored in your browser unless a cloud-sync feature explicitly tells you otherwise.

Your content and likeness

You keep ownership of content you create or upload, including your name, image, voice, persona, and other elements of your likeness. BIGLWA does not claim ownership merely because something is posted here. Any license you grant us is limited to what is reasonably necessary to host, display, deliver, moderate, back up, and operate the service.

What BIGLWA does not do

BIGLWA does not sell your personal information or creative work. BIGLWA does not intentionally use private user content or likenesses to train generative models without a separate, affirmative opt-in. Service providers may process data only as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy obligations.

Safety, evidence, and rights requests

If you report impersonation, nonconsensual use of your likeness, stolen creative work, doxxing, deepfake abuse, or another serious violation of your social body, BIGLWA may preserve relevant records, help organize evidence, assist with platform takedown requests, and escalate the matter under our Rights & Likeness process.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information BIGLWA controls, subject to security, fraud-prevention, legal, and backup-retention requirements. When publishing and cloud storage features expand, this policy should be updated before those new practices take effect.

Current-build note: this privacy policy describes the live BIGLWA prototype as it exists today. New messaging, posting, payments, cloud storage, analytics, or moderation systems will require corresponding updates.

Terms · September 7, 2026

You are not the product.

BIGLWA is a creative collective. Participation does not transfer ownership of your identity or your work to us.

Accounts and usernames

Public account creation currently requires a 5–24 character username. Three- and four-character usernames are reserved and may be assigned only through a special-request process. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for using an identity you have the right to use.

You own what you make

As between you and BIGLWA, you retain your rights in original content you create and post. You grant BIGLWA a limited, non-exclusive license only to operate the service: hosting, reproducing, displaying, transmitting, formatting, moderating, and backing up that content as reasonably necessary. This does not give BIGLWA permission to sell your work or independently commercialize your likeness.

No exploitation of another person’s social body

Do not impersonate people, scrape profiles for identity exploitation, publish nonconsensual intimate material, create deceptive or abusive synthetic likenesses, dox users, falsely claim authorship, or commercially exploit another person’s name, image, voice, persona, or work without a lawful basis.

Original work posted here

Where BIGLWA records publication metadata, that information may help document when a work appeared on the service. It is not a substitute for copyright registration, trademark registration, a contract, or other formal legal protection.

Enforcement and legal support

BIGLWA may remove violating content or accounts, preserve evidence, assist with takedown requests, and—where appropriate—support cease-and-desist correspondence. Matters that require legal representation or litigation must be handled by a licensed attorney under a separate written engagement. BIGLWA does not guarantee that every report will result in a cease-and-desist, lawsuit, recovery, or other legal remedy.

No automatic attorney-client relationship

Using BIGLWA, submitting a report, or receiving general support does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship. If BIGLWA later offers legal services through licensed counsel, the scope, jurisdiction, conflicts checks, fees if any, and representation terms must be set out separately.

These terms are a strong product-policy draft, not a substitute for review by counsel before a public commercial launch.

Rights & Likeness Promise

Your social body is part of your body.

Your face, voice, name, persona, archive, creative labor, and the context around them deserve meaningful protection. BIGLWA’s goal is to make that protection part of the product—not something you have to beg a platform for after harm is already done.

01 · You keep authorship

Posting here does not make BIGLWA the owner of your work.

02 · Likeness is not a free asset

Impersonation, abusive deepfakes, nonconsensual sexualization, and identity extraction are treated as serious violations.

03 · We help build the paper trail

When possible, we can help preserve evidence and organize the chronology of a reported violation.

04 · Escalation can be real

Depending on the facts, BIGLWA may support takedowns or cease-and-desist correspondence and may coordinate referral to licensed counsel.

When court becomes necessary

If a matter warrants litigation, representation must be accepted by a licensed attorney under a separate engagement. That protects you too: it ensures someone legally authorized, conflict-checked, and accountable is actually responsible for the case. BIGLWA can build toward that infrastructure without pretending every report automatically becomes a lawsuit.

Design principle: protection should be visible. As reporting and provenance tools are built, BIGLWA should show users what was preserved, what action was requested, and what stage a rights matter is in.

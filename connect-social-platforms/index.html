The social body belongs to the person

A social profile can contain photos, sounds, writing, relationships, time and memories. BIG LWA’s idea of the social body is broader than a follower count. Orbit helps you choose which connected services are useful and which things should stay separate.

Connecting a platform is optional. Authentication may require that provider's OAuth screen, and BIG LWA can access only the data covered by the permissions and supported endpoints. Revoking a connection stops new authorized access, but previously imported content may need separate removal from your BIG LWA account.

Getting started with Orbit

  1. Open Orbit

    Review the planets and their actual connection state.

  2. Choose one platform

    Review authorization permissions before proceeding; no need to connect everything.

  3. Check the results

    Inspect imported content in the relevant Archive, Boards, Stream or Calendar feature and make deliberate sharing choices.

Privacy should be the bare minimum.

BIG LWA does not own content because you connect or import it. Provider terms, copyright and data export limits still apply. Read the Privacy Policy and Rights & Likeness information before publishing.

Questions people ask

Can I connect multiple social accounts to BIG LWA?

Orbit presents multiple supported connections, but actual login, import and synchronization depend on each provider's permissions and current API access.

Does BIG LWA copy everything from my linked accounts?

No. Provider permissions limit what may be imported, and account linking does not guarantee every post, message, friend or video is available.

Can I disconnect a platform?

Supported connections have disconnect controls. You may also need to remove already-imported content separately.

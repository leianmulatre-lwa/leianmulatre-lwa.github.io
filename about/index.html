About BIGLWA

A space for everything
that makes you, you.

BIGLWA is a creative social networking platform bringing personal expression, visual inspiration, and cultural exploration into one workspace.

Explore BIGLWA

Created by Leian Stanley Mulatre, BIGLWA is for people whose interests cross disciplines: artists, students, storytellers, and curious minds.

The platform is in active development, shaped by a commitment to education, cultural preservation, and care for the people behind the work.

Make room for your interests.

01 / The experience

Studio

A personal workspace with a customizable profile and widgets for exploring different parts of your creative life.

Boards

A home for visual inspiration and references. Bring ideas for fashion, film, art, and other creative projects into focus.

Orbit

A place to bring your external profiles together. Account connections are being developed and tested, including a TikTok connection for profile information and public videos.

Your inspiration, connected.

02 / Pinterest & Boards
Planned integration · Not yet available

We are developing a Pinterest connection for Boards. The goal is to let you choose Pinterest boards you already use and view their Pins alongside your creative workspace.

A stylist could revisit references for a shoot. A filmmaker could collect a project's visual direction. A student could explore an art or design idea through their own collections.

Pinterest API access is being requested for development and testing. BIGLWA is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest.

How it is intended to work

  1. Choose to connect your Pinterest account and authorize access through Pinterest.
  2. Select the boards you want to view in BIGLWA.
  3. Browse their Pins, with links back to the original content on Pinterest.
  4. Disconnect your Pinterest account whenever you choose.

The initial scope is viewing authorized boards and Pins. Publishing Pins, scheduling posts, and managing advertisements are outside this initial integration.

03 / Our purpose

Founded by Leian Stanley Mulatre, a Haitian-born creator, creative director, and UCLA graduate, BIGLWA grows from an interest in how creativity, education, and community can support one another.

Its purpose is to help people express themselves, explore ideas with context, and build connections across interests and experiences.

Our Affidavit of Good Faith describes the mutual care and accountability we ask of the platform and its members.

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms for more information about participating in BIGLWA.

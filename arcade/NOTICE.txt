BIG LWA arcade preservation player
Ruffle 0.7.1: https://ruffle.rs/ | https://github.com/ruffle-rs/ruffle
The deployed runtime is fetched from the pinned official @ruffle-rs/ruffle npm package and checked with SHA-256. Ruffle license and package notices are preserved under /vendor/ruffle/.
Local Snake and Number Grid implementations and the Flash color-test fixture were created for BIG LWA. They are alternatives, not copies of the external games listed in the archive directory.
The Flash test cycles background colors to verify SWF playback. No third-party game ROMs, SWFs or BIOS files are bundled.

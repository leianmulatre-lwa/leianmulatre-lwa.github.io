01 / Account

Sign-in and email

Your email is how Big LWA reaches you about sign-ins, rights requests, and moderation decisions. It is never shown on your public profile.

You are signed out

Sign in to confirm your email, change it, or send yourself a password reset link.

Sign in
   

Changing your email is verified, not assumed. Big LWA sends a confirmation to the new address and only swaps it once you click that link, so nobody can take over your account by typing your name into a form.

Password reset links are single-use and expire. If you signed in with Google, Apple, or another provider, your password lives with that provider and there is nothing here to reset.

Why one place?

A photo can be a reference, a memory, a project, or a piece of your public profile. A song can sit alongside a journal entry; an article can turn into a research collection; a route can become a saved place. BIG LWA connects these activities while keeping private work separate from things you intentionally publish.

Some features sync to your signed-in BIG LWA account; others remain browser-based or require a provider connection. The Privacy Policy explains those differences. You don't need to link every account to use the platform.

Features are evolving.

BIG LWA is an actively developed platform. Search and discovery guides point to real features, not guaranteed 24/7 streams, live delivery prices, social-platform access or an automated multiplayer network. Specific capabilities depend on the connected provider and permissions.

Questions people ask

Can I try BIG LWA without connecting social accounts?

Yes. Many public guides are available without signing in, and account connections are optional. Certain Studio workspaces require a BIG LWA login.

Are the tools and online games completely free?

BIG LWA has browser tools and free game links. Third-party games, APIs and external services may have their own availability, accounts or terms.

Why are the features on different pages?

Each page explains a specific workflow and links to the relevant part of the Studio so you can tell what is active before entering.

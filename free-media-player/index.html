What free means here

The media player and discovery tools are free within BIG LWA; they do not guarantee commercial music licenses, provider access, or an unlimited song catalog. Playback depends on the source. You can browse radio, independent programming and media without purchasing a BIG LWA subscription.

BIG LWA does not charge a subscription to explore these core tools. Some features require a free sign-in. Third-party content, games, players, or imported media follow their own licensing, fees, availability, account requirements and terms. Tools and integrations are still developing.

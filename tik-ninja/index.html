What is tik.ninja?

The Sharpest TikTok Profile & Account Viewer on the Web

Tik.ninja is a free, anonymous TikTok viewer that lets you browse any public TikTok profile, watch videos, and view stories — all without a TikTok account and without anyone knowing you were there. No login. No app. No trace.

Enter any username and our TikTok profile viewer works as a full TikTok account viewer: see the account’s videos, follower count, total likes, bio, and reposts in one clean, distraction-free view. Whether you’re researching creators, keeping tabs on competitors, or just curious about an account, nothing you look at is ever linked back to you.

Want to watch stories too? Our dedicated TikTok story viewer lets you view stories from any public creator completely anonymously — they’ll never see your name in their viewer list.

TikTok Repost Viewer

Browse every public repost from any TikTok creator, anonymously. No login, no trace.

TikTok Comment Viewer

Read every comment and reply thread on any public TikTok video without a TikTok account.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tik.ninja, the free TikTok viewer and TikTok story viewer.

Is tik.ninja free to use?

Yes, completely. tik.ninja is a 100% free TikTok viewer. No paid plans, no subscriptions, no hidden fees.

Do I need a TikTok account to use tik.ninja?

No. Our tik tok viewer works without any TikTok account. Just visit tik.ninja and start browsing.

Can I view TikTok stories anonymously?

Yes. Our TikTok story viewer lets you watch stories without the creator knowing. Your identity is never revealed.

Can the TikTok user see that I viewed their profile?

No. tik.ninja has no way to associate the view with your identity. The profile owner will not see you in any viewer lists.

Can I use tik.ninja as a TikTok account viewer?

Yes. Enter any public username and tik.ninja works as a full TikTok account viewer. You can see the account’s videos, follower count, likes, bio, reposts, and stories without logging in and without being seen. It works for any public TikTok account, no app required.

Does tik.ninja work on mobile?

Yes. tik.ninja is fully responsive and works on all modern smartphones and tablets, no app required.

Can I view private TikTok accounts?

No. tik.ninja only displays publicly available content. Private accounts cannot be accessed through our TikTok viewer.

Is it possible to view someone’s TikTok reposts?

Yes. tik.ninja’s TikTok repost viewer lets you browse any public account’s reposts anonymously. Just enter a username, and the Reposts tab will show every video they’ve reshared with their followers.

Is tik.ninja affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance?

No. tik.ninja is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to TikTok or ByteDance.

Privacy Policy · September 7, 2026

Privacy should feel like shelter.

BIGLWA is designed around the idea that your identity, likeness, relationships, and creative work are not raw material for someone else to exploit.

What we collect right now

For account access, BIGLWA uses Firebase Authentication and may process your email address, username, authentication identifiers, and basic account status. Studio appearance choices such as wallpaper and widget styling are currently stored in your browser unless a cloud-sync feature explicitly tells you otherwise.

Your content and likeness

You keep ownership of content you create or upload, including your name, image, voice, persona, and other elements of your likeness. BIGLWA does not claim ownership merely because something is posted here. Any license you grant us is limited to what is reasonably necessary to host, display, deliver, moderate, back up, and operate the service.

What BIGLWA does not do

BIGLWA does not sell your personal information or creative work. BIGLWA does not intentionally use private user content or likenesses to train generative models without a separate, affirmative opt-in. Service providers may process data only as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy obligations.

Safety, evidence, and rights requests

If you report impersonation, nonconsensual use of your likeness, stolen creative work, doxxing, deepfake abuse, or another serious violation of your social body, BIGLWA may preserve relevant records, help organize evidence, assist with platform takedown requests, and escalate the matter under our Rights & Likeness process.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information BIGLWA controls, subject to security, fraud-prevention, legal, and backup-retention requirements. When publishing and cloud storage features expand, this policy should be updated before those new practices take effect.

Current-build note: this privacy policy describes the live BIGLWA prototype as it exists today. New messaging, posting, payments, cloud storage, analytics, or moderation systems will require corresponding updates.

Privacy Policy · Updated November 6, 2026

Privacy should be the bare minimum.

Your identity, likeness, relationships, and creative work are yours. They are not raw material for someone else to exploit. BIG LWA is being built around that principle, not treating it like a premium feature.

What it takes to make your Studio yours

BIG LWA uses Firebase Authentication for account access, including email addresses, usernames, authentication identifiers, and account status. Some profile settings, connected-account details, selected imports, posts, and saved media are stored through account-connected cloud services so they can be available beyond one browser. Other features still rely on browser storage for drafts, editor files, caches, previews, and preferences. Not every feature syncs between devices or saves automatically; follow each tool’s save, export, or publish controls.

Your work. Your name. Your likeness.

You keep ownership of content you create or upload, including your name, image, voice, persona, and other elements of your likeness. BIGLWA does not claim ownership merely because something is posted here. Any license you grant us is limited to what is reasonably necessary to host, display, deliver, moderate, back up, and operate the service.

Connected apps are choices, not ownership transfers

Orbit integrations may access account information, media, statistics, or other data that a connected service authorizes. The information available depends on the provider and the permissions granted. You can disconnect supported integrations in Orbit; disconnecting access does not necessarily erase content already imported into your BIG LWA account. You can request deletion through our data deletion process.

What BIG LWA does not do

BIGLWA does not sell your personal information or creative work. BIGLWA does not intentionally use private user content or likenesses to train generative models without a separate, affirmative opt-in. Service providers may process data only as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy obligations.

L’wazi AI: private pilot and consent

L’wazi is an optional research and creative assistance pilot. When you opt in to its AI response mode, your question and a limited set of public search-source titles, links, and snippets may be sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate an answer. The current pilot uses Cloudflare-hosted models, requires a signed-in authorized test account, and keeps connected private Studio journals, direct messages, private imports, and Orbit access tokens out of AI requests. Search for public sources may still contact other public services. L’wazi does not sell or rank answers for advertising, and its research results may be incomplete or mistaken.

Search-result controls let you hide a result for the current view or explicitly block an item, named source, or phrase. Your blocking choices and optional explanation are stored locally on this browser using an account-specific key when your account identity is available; they are not transmitted to L’wazi’s AI model, do not synchronize between devices, and can be reviewed or undone using “Manage blocked results.” SoundCloud music discovery opens its original search website and, only after you submit a public SoundCloud link, asks SoundCloud's official embed service for a player; SoundCloud may process requests under its own terms. Add to Tunes saves only a public track reference in account-keyed browser storage, not the underlying song or an R2 backup. Linked tracks do not sync between devices. Visual-Life jobs displays source-attributed listings from the separately run vendored JobSpy feed when available; original job boards control accuracy and availability, and the platform does not apply on your behalf. The pilot requests permission for the current browser session before sending questions to AI inference. Cloudflare processes submitted text under its applicable service terms; processing by a model provider is not the same as BIG LWA training a model on your personal work. BIG LWA does not use private writing to train or fine-tune L’wazi without a separate affirmative choice. The pilot is not yet a public feature, and provider availability, data practices, and controls may change before broader release.

Safety, evidence, and your rights

If you report impersonation, nonconsensual use of your likeness, stolen creative work, doxxing, deepfake abuse, or another serious violation of your social body, BIGLWA may preserve relevant records, help organize evidence, assist with platform takedown requests, and escalate the matter under our Rights & Likeness process.

Community profile and safety preferences

When you choose community roles, an age range, and a broad location in Edit Profile, these self-declared settings are saved to the owner-only Firebase account profile after server confirmation and can be restored on another device when you sign in. They are not proof of age or identity and are not collected through precise GPS. Previously saved browser-only settings can be reviewed and explicitly migrated by choosing Save to my account; the old browser copy is removed after a verified cloud save. Your role labels can appear on your own profile banner; age and location are kept out of public role badges. Room and Lounge drafts may be limited until these settings are supplied, and adult-only access cannot rely on self-declared information as verification.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information BIGLWA controls, subject to security, fraud-prevention, legal, and backup-retention requirements. Features and provider integrations change over time. We will update this policy to reflect significant changes to storage, sharing, or other data practices.

Current-build note: BIG LWA remains in active development. Some functions are live, some are limited previews, and some are still being built. A connected account, a local draft, and a published post are not the same thing. Please check each feature’s on-screen sharing and save options before using it.
